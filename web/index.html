Set up Claude for EBL work

Two minutes, once per computer. After this, anything you build with Claude for EBL is kept on the company shelf automatically.

1

Install the EBL skill

Open PowerShell (press Start, type PowerShell, Enter). Paste this line and press Enter:

irm https://ebl.sg/claude/install.ps1 | iex

You can also paste it into Claude Code and say "run this in PowerShell". Run it again any time to update.

2

Answer three questions

In the same window: your full name, your @ebl.sg email, and the six-digit code that arrives in that mailbox a few seconds later. Then close the window.

3

Wait for the nod

EBL is asked to approve your space. Once approved, your PC finishes setting itself up by itself within ten minutes. Nothing to type, nothing to install, nobody hands you a file.

✓

That is all

Work in Claude Code as you always do. Whatever you build for EBL is copied to the company shelf on its own after each turn and every night. Claude keeps a one-page description of each project current as it works. Nothing to remember, nothing to run.

Your chats are never sent. Personal projects send only a one-page note (name, owner, folder); their content stays on your PC. A file named .eblignore in a folder keeps that folder out entirely.

Handy phrases: ebl status shows what the shelf holds for this project, ebl sync off pauses everything on this PC, ebl sync on resumes.

ebl.sg/claude

Why this exists. EBL wants to keep the brain of every bot and automation built for the company, so that if you are away or move on, the company still knows what exists, how it works and how to rebuild it. The skill contains no passwords and no server details. Your space is approved by EBL, and your PC collects its own key. Questions: ask your manager.
